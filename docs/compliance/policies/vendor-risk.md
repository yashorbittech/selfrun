# Vendor and sub-processor risk policy

**Status: draft for adoption. Version 1.**

Before a supplier handles personal data: (1) record what data and why; (2) check its security attestation, region and breach-notice terms; (3) sign a data
processing agreement; (4) add it to `/subprocessors` and tell customers before it goes live (the DPA gives customers a way to object); (5) record any
transfer in [international-transfer-register](./international-transfer-register.md). Review each supplier yearly and when it reports an incident. Stop using a
supplier that cannot meet the terms, and make sure data is returned or deleted.
