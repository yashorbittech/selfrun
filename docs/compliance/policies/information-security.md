# Information security policy

**Status: draft for adoption by the owner. Version 1.** Owner of this policy: the person accountable for security (to be named).

1. **Purpose.** Protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of customer and platform data.
2. **Scope.** The application, its infrastructure, source code, staff and contractors, and suppliers that handle data.
3. **Principles.** Least privilege; deny by default; each company's data is isolated; security is checked before every release ([release criteria](../release-acceptance-criteria.md)); claims we make are true.
4. **Roles.** Security owner (policy, risk, incidents); engineering (secure development); data protection contact (requests, DPAs); every person (report concerns to the security owner at once).
5. **Requirements.** Access: [access-control-and-passwords](./access-control-and-passwords.md). Vulnerabilities: [vulnerability-management](./vulnerability-management.md). Suppliers: [vendor-risk](./vendor-risk.md). Incidents: [incident-response](./incident-response.md). Continuity: [business-continuity-dr](./business-continuity-dr.md). Data: [data-retention-deletion](./data-retention-deletion.md), [dsr-procedure](./dsr-procedure.md).
6. **Review.** At least yearly and after any serious incident. Breaches of the policy are handled as incidents and may lead to action under the person's agreement.
