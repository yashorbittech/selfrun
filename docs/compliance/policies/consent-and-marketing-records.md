# Consent and marketing records

**Status: draft for adoption. Version 1.**

- **Cookies and trackers.** Nothing non-essential loads until the visitor accepts; the choice is stored with a version and timestamp (`site_consent_records`, hashed visitor id, kept 3 years); withdrawing is as easy as accepting ("Cookie settings").
- **Re-ask** when the consent text version changes.
- **Marketing email.** Send only with opt-in or an existing customer relationship, with a working unsubscribe; keep the date, source and wording of each opt-in and every opt-out.
- **Purpose limits.** Data collected for one purpose is not reused for another without a new basis.
- **Customers' own websites.** The customer is the controller for visitors of its website; the platform supplies the banner and the records.
