# Data subject request procedure

**Status: draft for adoption. Version 1.**

1. **Intake.** `/data-requests` (form creates reference `PR-XXXXXXXX` and a 30-day due date) or the Grievance Officer's email. An acknowledgement is emailed within 24 hours.
2. **Verify identity** with information we already hold; do not ask for more than needed.
3. **Controller or processor?** If the data belongs to a customer company's records, forward the request to that company within 3 working days and tell the requester; we act on the company's instruction.
4. **Decide and act within 30 days** (summary of data under s. 11, correction, completion, updating and erasure under s. 12, grievance under s. 13, nomination under s. 14, withdrawal of consent). Resolve within 15 days where possible and in any case within 30 days; if more time is needed, tell the requester why before day 30.
5. **Refusal.** Give the reason and the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India after the grievance process, and for consumers the National Consumer Helpline (1915).
6. **Record** the outcome and keep the record.
