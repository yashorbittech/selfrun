# Data subject request procedure

**Status: draft for adoption. Version 1.**

1. **Intake.** `/data-requests` (form creates reference `PR-XXXXXXXX` and a 30-day due date) or the privacy email. An acknowledgement is emailed.
2. **Verify identity** with information we already hold; do not ask for more than needed.
3. **Controller or processor?** If the data belongs to a customer company's records, forward the request to that company within 3 working days and tell the requester; we act on the company's instruction.
4. **Decide and act within 30 days** (access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, withdraw consent; for India also nomination and grievance). One extension of up to 60 more days is allowed only where the law permits, with a reason sent to the requester.
5. **Refusal.** Give the reason and the right to complain to the regulator (ICO, Data Protection Board of India, UAE Data Office, SDAIA, state attorney general as applicable).
6. **Record** the outcome and keep the record.
